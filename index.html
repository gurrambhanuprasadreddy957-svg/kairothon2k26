About KAIROTHON

VEMU Institute of Technology

KAIROTHON 2K26 is a premium college-level hackathon designed to foster innovation, problem-solving, and creativity among future engineers. It brings together the brightest minds across multiple departments to tackle real-world challenges.

Vision: Empowering students to transform ideas into tangible technologies.

Objective: To provide a platform where creativity meets technology, resulting in actionable solutions for a sustainable and innovative future.
